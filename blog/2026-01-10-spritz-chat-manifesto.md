Communication is a universal human right recognized by the United Nations. Yet billions of people are denied meaningful access to it.

Nearly a third of the world does not have reliable access to voice or video communication. Families are separated not only by geography but by digital barriers. Geo blocking and censorship shut down conversations between people who simply want to speak to one another.

**We believe this is unacceptable.**

<!-- truncate -->

## The Problem with Centralized Communication

Modern communication platforms are built on centralized servers owned by centralized companies. These companies store conversations, read metadata, and control access. They decide who can communicate, where communication is allowed, and when it can be shut off.

Privacy is promised but rarely delivered.

Most companies optimize for the largest and easiest markets. They avoid regions with restrictions and build products for low resistance growth. True privacy and decentralization are treated as optional features instead of foundational principles.

People are told to rely on VPNs to bypass these barriers. But VPNs are blocked just as quickly as messaging and video apps. Temporary workarounds are not real solutions.

## Why Spritz Chat Exists

**Spritz Chat exists because this system is broken.**

Spritz Chat is a fully decentralized peer to peer communication network. There are no centralized servers and no gatekeepers. Conversations happen directly between people and are fully encrypted by design.

Your communication belongs to you alone. It is not stored, monitored, analyzed or sold. No company owns your conversations because communication should not be owned at all.

We believe real privacy requires real decentralization. That is why Spritz Chat is built on blockchain infrastructure to remove single points of control and failure.

## Intentional Trade-offs

This means we make intentional trade-offs:

- **We choose privacy over rapid growth.**
- **We choose decentralization over convenience.**
- **We choose long term resilience over short term scale.**

This path is harder. It does not optimize for speed or mass adoption. It does not follow existing business models.

But it serves people.

## Our Vision

Our vision is a connected future where anyone anywhere can communicate freely without permission, surveillance, or borders enforced by software.

Human connection should be peer to peer again.

**That is Spritz Chat. That is what we are building.**
